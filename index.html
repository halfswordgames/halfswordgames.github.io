A commoner. A blade. A tournament that wants you dead.

Half Sword is an immersive, physically simulated medieval combat game. You start as a commoner in 15th-century Europe and fight your way through brutal tournaments to earn a knight's place — one swing at a time.

Your mouse is the weapon

There are no canned attack animations. Your mouse drives the blade directly, so every cut, thrust and parry is something you did — and every mistake is yours too.

Arm yourself

Build a fighter that suits your style with authentic arms and armor: swords and shields, plate and helmets. What you wear changes how you move and what you can survive.

Duels and melees

One-on-one duels demand precision. Group battles demand awareness. Each mode asks for different tactics, and the arena is unforgiving either way.

What makes it different

  • Physics-driven ragdoll combat

    Bodies, limbs and weapons are simulated, not animated. Momentum, weight and balance decide the outcome of a clash — closer to a physical simulation than a combo system.

  • Historically styled arms and armor

    Weapons and harness modeled on 15th-century European equipment. Armor stops what it should stop, and leaves open what it should leave open.

  • A damage system that doesn't hold back

    Cuts go where the edge lands. Wounds slow you, bleed you and end you. The gore is heavy and it is the point: you feel the weight of every hit.

  • Duels and arena melees

    Face a single opponent or a crowd. Different modes, different rhythms, the same rule: stay standing.

The studio

Half Sword is being developed by Half Sword Games, a small studio based in Georgia, and published by Game Seer Publishing.

The team is geographically dispersed, with members in Georgia, Sweden, the Netherlands, India, and Argentina.
