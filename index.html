The studio

Half Sword Games is a small studio based in Georgia, founded in December 2023. Half Sword is published by Game Seer Publishing.

Our team operates without borders. With 14 developers collaborating across Georgia, Sweden, the Netherlands, India, Argentina, the UK, and other countries, development on Half Sword effectively happens around the clock.
